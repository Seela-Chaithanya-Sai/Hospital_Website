Medical Specialties

Our hospital offers expert medical care across multiple specialties, ensuring high-quality treatment with experienced doctors and advanced medical technology.

Our Departments

We provide specialized healthcare services through dedicated departments, each managed by highly qualified doctors and supported by modern medical equipment to ensure the best treatment for every patient.

Cardiology

Diagnosis and treatment of heart diseases, hypertension, heart failure, and cardiovascular disorders.

Neurology

Comprehensive care for disorders affecting the brain, spinal cord, and nervous system.

Orthopedics

Treatment for bone fractures, joint replacement, arthritis, sports injuries, and musculoskeletal disorders.

Pediatrics

Specialized healthcare services for infants, children, and adolescents.

Gynecology

Women's healthcare including pregnancy, reproductive health, and maternity services.

General Medicine

Diagnosis and treatment of common illnesses, preventive healthcare, and routine medical checkups.

Dermatology

Expert care for skin, hair, and nail disorders including allergies, infections, acne, eczema, and psoriasis.

Ophthalmology

Comprehensive eye care including vision testing, cataract treatment, glaucoma management, and eye surgery.

ENT

Diagnosis and treatment of ear, nose, and throat disorders using advanced medical techniques.

Dentistry

Complete dental care including cleaning, fillings, root canal treatment, cosmetic dentistry, and oral surgery.

Pulmonology

Specialized treatment for asthma, lung infections, COPD, breathing disorders, and respiratory diseases.

Emergency Medicine

24×7 emergency medical care for accidents, trauma, critical illnesses, and urgent medical conditions.
